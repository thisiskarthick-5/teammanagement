How to Use TeamSync Features

Dashboard Metrics & Analytics
The Dashboard displays real-time task stats (Total, Ended, Running, Pending). The Project Analytics bar chart tracks team activity across the days of the week, while Reminders and Project Progress cards keep your schedule on track.
Real-Time Team Chat
Use Team Chat to communicate with your team members in real time. Send project updates, share links, or post quick announcements to keep everyone aligned.
AI Smart Assistant
Click the floating green robot icon in the bottom right corner of any page to open the TeamSync AI Assistant. You can ask questions about your tasks, team members, attendance, or project statuses.
Settings & Profile Customization
Click Settings in the navigation sidebar to update your avatar, name, email, bio, and skills portfolio.
