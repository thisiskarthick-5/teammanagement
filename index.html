Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TeamSync features, security, and setup.

What is TeamSync and how does it help my team?
TeamSync is a collaborative team management platform that combines task assignment, proof-of-work verification, daily attendance tracking with streak multipliers, and an integrated Groq Llama-3.3 AI assistant to streamline team workflows.
How does the Groq AI writing helper work?
The Groq AI helper is integrated directly inside the team chat and smart assistant drawer. Powered by the ultra-fast Llama-3.3-70b model, it allows you to instantly rewrite messages into professional, concise, friendly, or custom tones, as well as answer workspace queries about tasks and attendance.
What is the Task Proof-of-Work Verification loop?
When team members complete a task, they submit a verification note and URL proof (e.g. GitHub PR, Figma design link, or build artifact). Admins can inspect the submission and either approve it (which increments user streaks) or request revisions with written feedback.
What is the difference between Admin and Member roles?
Admins have global workspace permissions to create tasks, approve or request revisions on task submissions, add or remove members, and access global analytics. Members have access to their personal dashboard, daily check-in calendar, current task lists, and team chat.
Can I try TeamSync for free?
Yes! The Starter plan is 100% free forever for teams up to 5 members. No credit card is required to sign up and get started.
Is my team data secure?
Yes. All authentication and data storage are powered by Google Firebase Firestore and Firebase Auth, employing industry-standard SSL encryption and strict security rules.
